# Notifications, accounts and settings

Sign up with your college email, username, roll number, department, year and a password of at least 8 characters. Verify your email through the link we send before logging in. If you forget your password use "Forgot your password" on the login page; the reset link works for one hour and once only.

The bell icon at the top right shows how many notifications are unread. A small card appears when something new arrives and then moves into the bell. All students receive new events and new recruitments. Announcements come from clubs you follow or belong to. Notifications about your own registrations, applications and approvals cannot be switched off.

In Settings you can change your password, switch notification categories on or off for the app and for email, and pick the light, dark or system theme.

Admin accounts (OSI, Events Authority and Super Admin) are created by a Super Admin and must change their first password at first login. Students cannot create new clubs yet; the OSI creates clubs and assigns the first Leader.
