# How events get approved

A club plans an event in its workspace under "Add an event". The event plan needs a title, summary, full description, objectives, date and time, mode (offline, online or hybrid), a registration deadline, the expected number of participants and a poster of at most 5 MB (PNG, JPG, WebP or PDF).

Only the club Leader can submit the event. When the Leader submits, the portal places a tentative hold on the chosen hall so nobody else can take it while the event waits for approval. A club can have at most two active holds at once.

The OSI reviews submitted events. It can approve and circulate the event, ask for changes (with a note) or reject it (with a note). After approval the event appears in the Events list, every student's calendar and in notifications. If changes are requested the club can edit the event and submit it again, and the hall hold is kept. If the event is rejected the hold is released straight away.

After approval a club member with the right role publishes the registration form. The seat limit starts at the capacity of the held hall. When registration closes, the Leader or Vice Leader sends the final hall request with the real number of registrations, and the Events Authority confirms the hall or chooses another one. Everyone registered is told the confirmed venue.
