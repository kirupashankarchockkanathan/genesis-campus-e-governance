# Joining clubs and recruitment

Every student can browse all clubs, favourite the ones they like and read their announcements. Favourite clubs appear on the Favourites page with their latest announcements, upcoming events and recent work.

To join a club, wait for it to open a recruitment. All students are notified when a recruitment opens, and the club page shows a Recruitment tab with the open positions and a Register button until the deadline. You apply once, answering the club's application form. You can withdraw your application before it is decided.

The club's Secretary, Vice Leader or Leader reviews applications and marks them shortlisted, accepted or rejected. You are notified of each change. When you are accepted you become a member with the Member role (or the role the reviewer chose) and the club appears under My Clubs. Some clubs switch on auto-accept, which makes you a member immediately.

Club roles are Leader, Vice Leader, Secretary, Event Coordinator, Media Lead and Member. What a person can do depends on their role inside that club only.
