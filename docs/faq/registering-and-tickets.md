# Registering for events and using your ticket

Open an event from the Events page and press Register. The form fills in your name, college email, roll number, department and year from your profile; you only answer the club's own questions. You can register once per event.

When you register you get a QR ticket on the event page and under Activities. Show it at the door: a club member scans it and you are marked as attended. If you register but do not attend, the event is labelled as missed in your past activities.

If the event is full the button says Full. Some clubs switch on a waitlist: you join it automatically, and if someone cancels the first student on the waitlist gets the seat and a notification. You can cancel your registration any time until the event starts, which frees the seat.

Each event page has a Share button with a link anyone can open without logging in. Registering always needs a login.

Your calendar shows every approved event of every club. Registrations are marked, and you can export your personal calendar feed to other calendar apps from Settings.
